Sportico was known for breaking sports business news. In Doha, it set out to become the place where that news gets made, and the room had to say so before anyone spoke.

Turn a newsroom into a host. Bring leaders from sport, media, politics and investment together, and give them a reason to come back.

Give the summit a signature. A mark, a palette and type of its own, so every screen, sign and stage read as one experience.

Let Qatar be felt in every room. Majlis lounges under woven canopies, carved arches and a living green corridor brought Qatari culture onto an international stage.

Point every step. Arrival routes, wayfinding and stage design kept a small room of very big names moving, from the conference hall to the arena.

The room. Seventy-five guests over three days, including Barack Obama, Tony Blair, Joe Tsai, Ari Emanuel, Magic Johnson and Ryan Reynolds, with Lang Lang playing at the Museum of Islamic Art.

Sportico arrived in Doha as a news outlet and left as a convener, with the summit set to return every year. A newsroom that became the room.
