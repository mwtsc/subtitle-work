Islamic Relief USA spent decades earning trust by responding to emergencies. Over time, its work grew into education, livelihoods and long term development, and the name no longer reflected that range.

Challenge

The name pointed to relief, while much of the work had moved toward long term development. Changing it carried real risk to the trust donors had built over many years.

Opportunity

A new name could hold the full scope of the mission and invite supporters into what comes next, with its faith and history still in view.

Solution

Subtitle led the rebrand and the website build: the rename to IDEA, Islamic Development and Empowerment in America, a new identity system and messaging, and a site shaped around the organization's development work.

Rebranding

A new name, an identity built on meaning, and messaging that speaks to the organization's full mission.

Naming

IDEA keeps faith at the center of the name and gives the organization's development work room to grow.
