For three decades, Islamic Relief USA answered the world's emergencies. But the work had already moved past them, into schools, livelihoods and long term development. The name hadn't. It still said relief, and only relief.

The brief came down to one line: give the organization a name that matches the work it actually does, without putting three decades of donor trust at risk.

We led the rename from strategy to launch. IDEA, Islamic Development and Empowerment in America, keeps the faith in the name and makes room for everything the organization had grown into.

Then we built everything the name needed to live: the identity system, the messaging, the website and the launch campaign. One brief. One shot. No soft rollout.

Relief is a response.
Development is a promise.

The shift the organization made is built straight into the palette. Two blues, one direction.
