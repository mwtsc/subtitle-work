The name was
the ceiling.

Thirty years of credibility. Operations across 40+ countries. A mission that was always universal. But the name said otherwise: too narrow for the scope, too easy to misread.

The organization needed a name that could carry the weight of what it had become. Not just what it responded to, but what it was building toward.

Islamic Relief USA had spent three decades building trust through humanitarian work around the world. But the moment had arrived to signal something larger: a shift from emergency response toward long-term development and empowerment. A name that opened doors instead of narrowing them.

Subtitle led the complete strategic rename. We worked with leadership to stress-test every option, build the narrative rationale, and land on IDEA: Islamic Development and Empowerment in America. A name with meaning at every level, clear without being hollow.

From there we built the full identity system: visual language, typography, color, motion principles. Then came the messaging architecture, the new website, and a launch campaign. One brief. One shot. No soft rollout.
